The two refusals

A chatbot that is usually right is no use here.

A buyer repeats your number to their bank. One invented figure is the brokerage's problem, not the model's — so the model is never the source of one.

It cannot invent a price

Every figure is read from the brokerage's own inventory file. An outbound guard holds the set of numbers those facts support, and a reply carrying anything else never leaves the process — it becomes a handover instead.

model wrote
"around AED 1,250,000, you'll see 9% yield"
customer received
"Let me have an agent confirm the exact figures and call you."

It gives no investment advice

Yield, appreciation, whether an area is going up, whether to buy — always a person. Checked before the price lookup, so an ROI question hiding inside a price question still reaches a human. That is RERA exposure, not politeness.

asked
"what's the ROI on a 2 bed in JVC and how much is it?"
routed
handover · reason: advice
What the sales floor gets

One line, with the reason attached.

Five signals, read out of whatever the buyer volunteers. Asking again for something they just said is the fastest way to lose a chat, so one message can fill all five. A grade nobody can predict is one the floor stops trusting, so every grade carries why.

Under it

Built so the dangerous things are impossible.

campaigns
Nothing is created active. Campaigns, ad sets and ads are written PAUSED with no override; activating is a separate call that logs a warning.
budgets
Minor units, checked. A float raises TypeError — 50.0 means AED 50 to a person and AED 0.50 to Meta.
budget rules
Deciding is separate from doing. Evaluation is a pure function; applying defaults to a dry run, needs a minimum sample before forming an opinion, and moves a budget by one bounded step at most.
conversions
Personal data is SHA-256'd after normalising, with no flag to skip it, and every event carries an id so the pixel and the server deduplicate. Without that, cost per lead reads at half its real value.
retries
Selective. Meta answers a rate limit and your own malformed request with the same HTTP 400, so only the documented transient codes are retried.
